Reading the plate

Free evolution predicts where a detection is likely, and repeated clicks trace that distribution. It does not select the click in a single run; Hossenfelder’s “half-information” ceiling is a separate claim about access to a complex-valued state through real-valued measurements, not a 50% chance of a click.
