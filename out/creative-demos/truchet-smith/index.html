Truchet Tiles

Truchet tiling — Smith quarter-arc contours

A 16×16 grid in which each cell carries two quarter-circle arcs in a coin-flip orientation; adjacent arcs join into continuous meanders across the whole field.

Paper / citationSébastien Truchet, “Mémoire sur les combinaisons,” Mém. Acad. Roy. Sci. (1704); Cyril Stanley Smith, “The Tiling Patterns of Sebastian Truchet and the Topology of Structural Hierarchy,” Leonardo 20(4):373–385 (1987).
